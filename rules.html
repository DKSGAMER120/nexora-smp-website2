NEXORA SMP COMMUNITY

SERVER RULES

Follow these rules to keep Nexora SMP fair, enjoyable and welcoming for everyone.

📌 Important: By playing on Nexora SMP, you agree to follow the server rules and reasonable staff instructions. Rules may be updated when necessary, so check this page regularly.
01
🤝

Respect Everyone

Treat other players and staff respectfully. Keep the community friendly and enjoyable.

  • No harassment or targeted abuse.
  • No threats or intimidation.
  • Do not deliberately disrupt another player's experience.
02
💬

Keep Chat Appropriate

Use Minecraft and Discord chat responsibly.

  • No excessive spam.
  • Do not flood chat with repeated messages.
  • Avoid inappropriate content.
03
🛠️

No Exploits

Do not abuse bugs, exploits or unintended mechanics to gain an unfair advantage.

  • Report serious bugs to staff.
  • Do not duplicate items using exploits.
  • Do not intentionally abuse server errors.
04
🔐

Account Security

Keep your account credentials private and protect your account.

  • Never share your password.
  • Do not attempt to access another player's account.
  • Report suspicious account activity to staff.
05
🏠

Respect Builds

Respect player bases and community builds.

  • Do not intentionally destroy another player's build.
  • Do not build specifically to obstruct another player.
  • Respect protected areas.
06
📢

No Advertising

Keep outside-server advertising out of Nexora's chat and community spaces.

  • No unsolicited server advertising.
  • No repeated promotional messages.
  • Follow the Discord advertising rules as well.
07
👮

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff members help maintain the server and investigate rule violations.

  • Follow reasonable staff requests.
  • Do not deliberately obstruct an investigation.
  • Use the support system for appeals.
08
⚖️

Fair Play

Play Nexora using the server's intended gameplay systems.

  • Do not use prohibited client modifications or unfair tools.
  • Do not exploit unintended server behavior.
  • Do not manipulate server systems unfairly.
🌐

Discord Rules

The Nexora Discord has its own community guidelines. Follow the rules shown there as well.

🏪

Economy Fairness

Do not manipulate shops, trades or economy systems through exploits or unintended behavior.

🐛

Report Bugs

If you discover a serious bug, report it instead of abusing it for personal advantage.

Need Help or Want to Report Something?

If you have a question about a rule, need staff assistance or want to report an issue, contact the Nexora community through Discord.
